## Common questions

### Where are my memories stored?

On your iPhone, in Orbit's private app sandbox. If you have iCloud
Drive enabled, they also sync to your private iCloud so you can pick
up across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. We never see them.

See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the full picture.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone (not Orbit — iOS Settings).
2. Tap your name at the top → **Subscriptions**.
3. Tap **Orbit** → **Cancel Subscription**.

You'll keep Pro features through the end of the current billing
period.

### How do I delete my account?

Inside Orbit, go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This
wipes every memory you've captured, signs you out, and resets the app
to its first-launch state. The action can't be undone.

### Why didn't my voice note transcribe?

Orbit transcribes voice notes on-device using Apple's Speech
Recognition. iOS will ask for permission the first time you record.
If you previously declined, go to **iOS Settings → Orbit → Speech
Recognition** and turn it on. Existing voice notes will transcribe
the next time you open them.

### Does Orbit work without internet?

Yes. Capturing, browsing your timeline, voice transcription, and all
AI features run on-device. iCloud sync requires a network when it
runs but is not required for the app to function.

### Which iPhones run the AI features?

The AI surfaces (Daily Recap, Ask Orbit, auto-summaries) require an
iPhone with **Apple Intelligence** — iPhone 15 Pro / Pro Max,
iPhone 16 lineup, or newer, running iOS 18.1 or later. Older devices
get lightweight heuristic versions of the same features.

### How does the Apple Watch app work?

Orbit on Apple Watch captures voice notes. Tap the mic, talk, tap to
stop. The audio transfers to your iPhone automatically the next time
both devices are reachable, and the memory appears in your timeline
once Orbit ingests it.

### What does my data look like inside Apple Calendar and Reminders?

When you turn on Calendar sync, Orbit creates a dedicated "Orbit"
calendar in your Calendar app, and mirrors any task with a due date
there. When you turn on Reminders sync, every Orbit task lands in
your default Reminders list. Removing the sync toggle stops new
events / reminders from being written; existing ones stay until you
delete the Orbit calendar or list.

### My screen reader / Larger Text setting looks off

Please email us with the device + iOS version. Orbit is designed to
respect Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion across every
screen — if a surface isn't honoring your accessibility setting,
that's a bug we want to fix.

## Report a bug

Email **[support@orbitbrain2.netlify.app](mailto:support@orbitbrain2.netlify.app)**
with:

1. A short description of what went wrong.
2. What you were doing when it happened.
3. Your device + iOS version (Settings → General → About).
4. (Optional) A screenshot or screen recording.

We triage every report and ship fixes in regular updates.
