## Built natively for every Apple surface

A horizontal scroll or grid:

- **Share Sheet** — send into Orbit from any app
- **Reminders & Calendar Sync** — tasks land in iOS where you live
- **Apple Intelligence** — the AI you already trust, on your device

## Privacy isn't a checkbox

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Where does my data live? | On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud. We never see it. |
| Where does the AI run? | On-device. Apple Intelligence handles every prompt locally. |
| Do you track me? | No. No analytics SDKs. No advertising IDs. No cross-app tracking. |
| Can I delete everything? | Yes — Settings → Account → Delete account wipes the whole experience. |
